FFmpegFreeUI Native is a native C++ rewrite derived from Lake1059/FFmpegFreeUI.
Upstream snapshot: 65aec1ff4dcd62a54c4361fe1719520378750c36 (version 6.2.36).
The preset field names, built-in preset data and app icon originate from that
project. Its MIT license and copyright notice are preserved in LICENSES/Upstream-MIT.txt.

https://github.com/Lake1059/FFmpegFreeUI
New C++ code is licensed under AGPL-3.0-only; see LICENSE.txt.

Qt 6 Core, Gui and Widgets are dynamically linked. Qt is available under
LGPL-3.0 / GPL-3.0 / commercial licenses; this build uses the LGPL components.
https://www.qt.io/licensing/open-source-lgpl-obligations
Qt license texts: https://code.qt.io/cgit/qt/qtbase.git/tree/LICENSES
On Linux Qt is supplied by the distribution, including Wayland and XCB plugins.

FFmpeg / ffprobe / ffplay are external programs. They are not included in Linux
packages. Their licenses and codec availability depend on the installed build.
https://ffmpeg.org/legal.html

LakeUI, .NET and proprietary Windows support modules are not included.
